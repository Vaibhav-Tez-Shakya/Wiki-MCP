# Money Lending and Other Sins I

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2  
**Tier:** 1  
**Chapter:** Chapter 2 — Horseshoe Overlook  
**Mission Type:** Camp Mission  
**Location:** Horseshoe Overlook and surrounding area  
**Order:** Chapter 2

## Overview

Leopold Strauss sends Arthur to collect debts owed to the Van der Linde gang.

## Objectives

- Speak with Strauss.
- Accept the debt collection task.
- Locate the debtor.
- Collect the outstanding debt.
- Return to camp.

## Walkthrough

Strauss explains that several people owe money to the gang. Arthur is sent to collect one of the debts and learns more about the gang's financial operations.

Arthur collects the debt and returns to camp.

## Characters

- Arthur Morgan
- Leopold Strauss
- Debt collector target

## Location

Horseshoe Overlook and surrounding settlements.

## Rewards

Money for the gang and camp progression.

## Mission Outcome

Arthur collects an outstanding debt for Strauss.

## Branch Relationship

Beginning of the Money Lending and Other Sins mission chain.
