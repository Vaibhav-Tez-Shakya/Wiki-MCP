# Who is Not Without Sin

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2  
**Tier:** 1  
**Chapter:** Chapter 2 — Horseshoe Overlook  
**Mission Type:** Camp Mission  
**Location:** Valentine and surrounding area  
**Order:** Chapter 2

## Overview

Arthur searches for Reverend Swanson after learning that he has disappeared while drinking and gambling.

## Objectives

- Search for Reverend Swanson.
- Locate him near Valentine.
- Deal with the men confronting him.
- Help Swanson.
- Return him toward camp.

## Walkthrough

Arthur searches for Reverend Swanson and discovers him in trouble after a night of drinking and gambling.

Arthur intervenes, helps Swanson escape his predicament, and takes him back toward camp.

- Reverend Swanson
- Valentine residents
