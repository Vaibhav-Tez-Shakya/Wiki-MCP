# Exit Pursued by a Bruised Ego

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2  
**Tier:** 1  
**Chapter:** Chapter 2 — Horseshoe Overlook  
**Mission Type:** Main Story Mission  
**Location:** Valentine and surrounding wilderness  
**Order:** Chapter 2

## Overview

Arthur helps Hosea hunt a legendary bear while learning more about hunting and tracking.

## Objectives

- Meet Hosea.
- Travel to the hunting area.
- Track the bear.
- Hunt the bear.
- Decide whether to continue hunting it.
- Return to camp or continue the hunt.

## Walkthrough

Arthur meets Hosea and joins him on a hunting expedition. During the journey, Hosea introduces Arthur to tracking and prepares him for an encounter with a legendary bear.

Arthur and Hosea locate the bear. After the encounter, the two men separate depending on Arthur's decision to continue pursuing it.

## Characters

- Arthur Morgan
- Hosea Matthews

## Location

Wilderness near Valentine.

## Rewards

Hunting progression and access to the Legendary Bear hunt.

## Mission Outcome

Arthur learns advanced hunting and tracking techniques and encounters the Legendary Bear.

## Branch Relationship

Main Chapter 2 mission.
