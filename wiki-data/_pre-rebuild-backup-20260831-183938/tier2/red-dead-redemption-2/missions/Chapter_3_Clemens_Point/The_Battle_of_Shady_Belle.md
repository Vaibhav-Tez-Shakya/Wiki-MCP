# The Battle of Shady Belle

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2
**Tier:** 1
**Chapter:** Chapter 3 — Clemens Point
**Mission Type:** Main Story Mission
**Location:** Shady Belle
**Branch:** None

## Overview

Arthur and John clear Shady Belle of hostile gang members so the Van der Linde gang can establish a new camp.

## Objectives

- Travel to Shady Belle.
- Search the house.
- Eliminate the hostile gang members.
- Secure the property.
- Return for the gang.

## Walkthrough

Arthur and John investigate Shady Belle and discover that it is occupied by hostile members of the Lemoyne Raiders.

They clear the property and prepare it for the gang's relocation.

## Characters

- Arthur Morgan
- John Marston
- Lemoyne Raiders

## Location

Shady Belle.

## Rewards

Story progression and new camp location.

## Mission Outcome

Shady Belle becomes the gang's new base.

## Branch Relationship

Leads into Chapter 4.
