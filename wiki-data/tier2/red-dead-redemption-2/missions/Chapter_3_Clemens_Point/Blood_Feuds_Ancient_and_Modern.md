# Blood Feuds, Ancient and Modern

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2
**Tier:** 1
**Chapter:** Chapter 3 — Clemens Point
**Mission Type:** Main Story Mission
**Location:** Braithwaite Manor
**Branch:** None

## Overview

The Van der Linde gang attacks the Braithwaite estate after the kidnapping of Jack Marston.

## Objectives

- Gather the gang.
- Travel to Braithwaite Manor.
- Attack the estate.
- Search for Jack.
- Rescue Jack.

## Walkthrough

Arthur joins the gang in a coordinated assault on Braithwaite Manor.

The gang fights through the estate and confronts the Braithwaite family before discovering Jack's whereabouts.

Jack is recovered and the gang's conflict with the Braithwaites reaches its climax.
