## Chapter Overview

The gang moves south and east after being driven out of Valentine. They settle near Rhodes and become involved in the conflict between the Gray and Braithwaite families.

The source data contains multiple mission groups. The mission names are normalized into unique mission records, while the original branch/group structure and source-listed OR relationships are preserved separately.

## Normalization Rules Applied

- Each unique mission name is stored as one mission record.
- Original mission groups are preserved.
- Source-listed OR relationships are preserved.
- The Course of True Love I remains identifiable as the first part of its sequence.
- No additional missions are invented.
- No source mission reference is silently removed.
- Obvious source formatting errors are corrected without changing meaning.
- Source relationships are preserved without adding unsupported gameplay interpretations.
