# American Distillation

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2
**Tier:** 1
**Chapter:** Chapter 3 — Clemens Point
**Mission Type:** Main Story Mission
**Location:** Rhodes and surrounding countryside
**Branch:** None

## Overview

Arthur works with the Rhodes lawmen to raid a moonshine operation.

## Objectives

- Meet the lawmen.
- Travel to the moonshine operation.
- Raid the camp.
- Destroy the still.
- Escape the area.

## Walkthrough

Arthur assists the authorities in disrupting a moonshine operation.

The raid strengthens the gang's position in Rhodes while creating additional complications with local criminal groups.

- Sheriff Gray
- Moonshiners

Money and story progression.

The moonshine operation is disrupted.
