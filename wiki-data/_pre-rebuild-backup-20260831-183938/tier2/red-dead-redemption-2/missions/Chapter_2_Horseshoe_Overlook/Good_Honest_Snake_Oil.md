# Good, Honest, Snake Oil

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2
**Tier:** 1
**Chapter:** Chapter 2 — Horseshoe Overlook
**Mission Type:** Stranger Mission
**Location:** Valentine
**Branch:** None

## Overview

Arthur helps Valentine authorities pursue a wanted criminal while becoming involved with a fraudulent medicine salesman.

## Objectives

- Speak with the sheriff.
- Track the wanted man.
- Capture the target.
- Return the prisoner.
- Deal with the snake oil salesman.

## Walkthrough

Arthur accepts a bounty-related task from the sheriff in Valentine. He tracks down the wanted criminal and brings him back.

The mission also introduces Arthur to a fraudulent snake oil salesman operating in the region.

## Characters

- Arthur Morgan
- Valentine Sheriff
- Wanted criminal
- Snake oil salesman

## Location

Valentine and surrounding countryside.

## Rewards

Bounty payment and story progression.

## Mission Outcome

Arthur captures the wanted man and completes the sheriff's assignment.

## Branch Relationship

Standalone mission.
