# Dragon's Dogma 2 — Game Systems

## Leveling and XP

Dragon's Dogma 2 uses a character-level progression system.

- Character level starts at 1.
- Experience Points (XP) are primarily gained through quests and defeating enemies.
- XP automatically contributes to character leveling.
- The XP requirement increases as character level increases.
- Character level affects the growth of core attributes such as Health, Stamina, Strength, Magick, Defense, and Magick Defense.
- Leveling is separate from Vocation Rank progression.
- XP and Discipline Points are both gained through gameplay, but they serve different progression purposes.

## Character Progression

Character progression is composed of several interacting systems:

- Character Level
- Vocation
- Vocation Rank
- Discipline Points
- Skills
- Core Abilities
- Equipment
- Character Attributes

Character level primarily controls general stat growth, while Vocation Rank controls access to vocation-specific skills and augments.

## Vocation System

Vocations define the character's combat role and available equipment, skills, and abilities.

The player can change vocation during the game, allowing different combat roles and progression paths.

Vocation progression is separate from character level progression.

## Vocation Rank

Each vocation has its own progression rank.

- Vocation Rank starts at 1.
- Maximum documented Vocation Rank is 9.
- Discipline Points are used to unlock vocation skills, augments, and progression.
- Rank progression is specific to the vocation being developed.
- Changing vocation does not reset the character's overall level.

## Discipline Points

Discipline Points are a separate progression resource from XP.

They are used to:

- Learn vocation skills
- Learn augments
- Unlock or develop vocations
- Increase Vocation Rank

Discipline Points are earned alongside experience from gameplay.

## Skills and Abilities

Skills and augments are strongly connected to Vocation Rank and Discipline Points.

Higher Vocation Ranks unlock additional abilities and augments.

Therefore, character level alone does not completely represent combat capability.

## Equipment Progression

Weapons and armor contribute significantly to combat effectiveness.

Equipment progression interacts with:

- Character level
- Vocation
- Skills
- Enemy type
- Exploration
- Upgrade materials

A higher character level does not automatically mean maximum combat effectiveness if equipment and vocation progression are behind.

## Combat Progression

Combat effectiveness depends on multiple systems:

- Character Level
- Vocation
- Vocation Rank
- Skills
- Core Abilities
- Equipment
- Party composition
- Enemy type
- Player execution

This means DD2 does not have a simple "character level = total power" model.

## Difficulty Scaling

Dragon's Dogma 2 does not primarily use a conventional selectable difficulty curve for normal progression.

Difficulty can vary substantially according to:

- Enemy type
- Location
- Encounter
- Equipment
- Character progression
- Player preparation
- Exploration

Exploration can expose the player to stronger encounters before the player is otherwise prepared for them.

## Quest Progression

Quests contribute to:

- Story progression
- XP
- Rewards
- Exploration
- Character progression

Quest difficulty is affected by the location, enemies, equipment, vocation, and player preparation.

The wiki should not infer a recommended character level for a quest unless that level is explicitly documented.

## World Progression

World progression is connected to:

- Main quests
- Side quests
- Locations
- NPC interactions
- Exploration
- Vocation progression
- Equipment progression

The open-world structure means players can encounter content outside a simple linear level sequence.

## Resource Systems

Important progression resources include:

- Experience Points (XP)
- Discipline Points
- Gold
- Health
- Stamina
- Weapons
- Armor
- Consumables
- Upgrade materials
- Rift-related resources

XP and Discipline Points should be treated as separate progression currencies.

## Endgame Progression

Endgame progression can involve:

- Continued character leveling
- Vocation development
- Skill and augment acquisition
- Equipment upgrades
- Stronger encounters
- Additional exploration
- New-cycle or post-story activities

Exact endgame level requirements should only be recorded when supported by a source.
