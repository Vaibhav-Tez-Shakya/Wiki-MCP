# A Fisher of Men

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2  
**Tier:** 1  
**Chapter:** Chapter 2 — Horseshoe Overlook  
**Mission Type:** Main Story Mission  
**Location:** Dakota River and Flat Iron Lake  
**Order:** Chapter 2

## Overview

Arthur takes Jack Marston fishing near the Dakota River and Flat Iron Lake while helping the young boy spend time away from camp.

## Objectives

- Travel with Jack to the fishing location.
- Teach Jack how to fish.
- Catch a fish.
- Return toward camp.
- Deal with the lawmen encountered during the return.

## Walkthrough

Arthur takes Jack fishing and introduces him to the activity. The peaceful outing changes when Arthur and Jack encounter lawmen while returning.

Arthur protects Jack and deals with the resulting confrontation before returning to camp.

## Characters

- Arthur Morgan
- Jack Marston
- Andrew Milton
- Edgar Ross

## Location

Dakota River and surrounding area.

## Rewards

Fishing tutorial and story progression.

## Mission Outcome

Arthur and Jack return from their fishing trip after Arthur protects Jack during the encounter with the lawmen.

## Branch Relationship

This is a Chapter 2 main-story mission associated with the camp's activities at Horseshoe Overlook.
