# Angelo Bronte, a Man of Honor

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2
**Tier:** 1
**Chapter:** Chapter 4 — Saint Denis
**Mission Type:** Main Story Mission
**Location:** Saint Denis
**Branch:** None

## Overview

Arthur, Dutch and John meet Angelo Bronte while negotiating the return of Jack Marston.

## Objectives

- Meet Angelo Bronte.
- Negotiate for Jack.
- Complete Bronte's task.
- Return Jack to the gang.

## Walkthrough

The gang meets Angelo Bronte, a powerful figure in Saint Denis's criminal underworld.

Bronte agrees to return Jack in exchange for the gang completing a task for him.

## Characters

- Arthur Morgan
- Dutch van der Linde
- John Marston
- Angelo Bronte
- Jack Marston

## Location

Saint Denis.

## Rewards

Jack's return and story progression.

## Mission Outcome

Jack is returned to the gang and Bronte becomes an important contact.

## Branch Relationship

Central Chapter 4 storyline.
