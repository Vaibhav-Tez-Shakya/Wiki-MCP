# Old Friends

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2  
**Tier:** 1  
**Chapter:** Chapter 1 — Colter  
**Mission Type:** Main Story Mission  
**Order:** 4  
**Location:** Colter and O'Driscoll camp  
**Branch:** Main Story

## Overview

Arthur rides with Dutch and Micah to locate an O'Driscoll camp. The gang attacks the O'Driscolls while searching for information and supplies.

## Objectives

- Ride with Dutch and Micah.
- Search for the O'Driscoll camp.
- Approach the camp.
- Attack the O'Driscolls.
- Eliminate the hostile gang members.
- Search the camp.
- Return to Colter.

## Walkthrough

Arthur joins Dutch and Micah as they travel through the snowy wilderness looking for members of the O'Driscoll gang.

The group discovers an O'Driscoll camp and launches an attack. Arthur participates in the firefight and helps eliminate the hostile gang members.

After the camp is secured, Arthur and the others search the area for supplies and information before returning to the gang's camp at Colter.

## Characters

- Arthur Morgan
- Dutch van der Linde
- Micah Bell
- O'Driscoll gang members

## Location

An O'Driscoll camp near Colter.

## Rewards

Loot and supplies obtained from the camp, along with story progression.

## Mission Outcome

The Van der Linde gang strikes against the O'Driscolls and obtains useful supplies while continuing its efforts to survive at Colter.

## Branch Relationship

This mission is part of the Chapter 1 main-story progression.
