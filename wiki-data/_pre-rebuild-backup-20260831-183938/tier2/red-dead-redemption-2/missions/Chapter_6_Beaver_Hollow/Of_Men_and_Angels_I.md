# Of Men and Angels I

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2
**Tier:** 1
**Chapter:** Chapter 6 — Beaver Hollow
**Mission Type:** Stranger Mission

## Overview

Arthur assists Sister Calderón with charitable work in Saint Denis.

- Meet Sister Calderón.
- Help the poor.

- Deliver assistance.

Arthur encounters Sister Calderón and assists with charitable work among the poor in Saint Denis.

The mission highlights Arthur's growing willingness to help people outside the gang.
