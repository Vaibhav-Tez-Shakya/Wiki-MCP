# The First Shall Be Last

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2  
**Tier:** 1  
**Chapter:** Chapter 2 — Horseshoe Overlook  
**Mission Type:** Main Story Mission  
**Location:** Dakota River and Blackwater region  
**Order:** Chapter 2

## Overview

Arthur, Javier, and Charles search for Sean MacGuire after learning that he has been captured by bounty hunters.

## Objectives

- Meet Javier and Charles.
- Travel toward the search area.
- Locate Sean.
- Attack the bounty hunters.
- Rescue Sean.
- Escape the area.

## Walkthrough

Arthur joins Javier and Charles in searching for Sean, who has been captured by bounty hunters.

The group locates the captors, attacks them, and frees Sean before returning toward camp.

## Characters

- Arthur Morgan
- Javier Escuella
- Charles Smith
- Sean MacGuire
- Bounty hunters

## Location

Dakota River and surrounding area.

## Rewards

Sean's return to the gang and story progression.

## Mission Outcome

Sean MacGuire is rescued and returns to the Van der Linde gang.
