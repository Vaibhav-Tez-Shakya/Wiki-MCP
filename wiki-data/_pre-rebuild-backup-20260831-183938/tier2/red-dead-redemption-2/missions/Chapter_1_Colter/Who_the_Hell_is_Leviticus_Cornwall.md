# Who the Hell is Leviticus Cornwall?

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2  
**Tier:** 1  
**Chapter:** Chapter 1 — Colter  
**Mission Type:** Main Story Mission  
**Order:** 5  
**Location:** Near Colter and the train route  
**Branch:** Main Story

## Overview

The Van der Linde gang plans a train robbery targeting a train owned by wealthy industrialist Leviticus Cornwall.

## Objectives

- Travel with Dutch and the gang.
- Reach the train route.
- Prepare the ambush.
- Stop the train.
- Rob the train.
- Defeat the guards.
- Search the train for valuables and supplies.
- Escape the area.

## Walkthrough

Dutch organizes a train robbery after the gang learns that a train belonging to Leviticus Cornwall will pass through the area.

Arthur joins the gang as they prepare an ambush. The gang stops the train and attacks the guards while searching the train cars for money, weapons and supplies.

The robbery provides the gang with much-needed resources while also creating a direct conflict with Cornwall and his interests.

## Location

The snowy wilderness near Colter and the train route.

## Rewards

Money, valuables, supplies and story progression.

## Mission Outcome

The gang successfully robs Cornwall's train and obtains resources needed for survival, but the robbery increases the gang's exposure and creates new enemies.

## Branch Relationship

This mission is part of the main Chapter 1 progression and leads toward the gang's eventual departure from Colter.
