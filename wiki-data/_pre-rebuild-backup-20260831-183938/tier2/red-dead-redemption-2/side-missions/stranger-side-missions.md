# Red Dead Redemption 2 — Stranger Side Missions

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2
**Tier:** 1
**Section:** Stranger Side Missions

## Overview

Red Dead Redemption 2 contains numerous Stranger side-missions that can be encountered throughout the main story.

The mission system includes Stranger missions as well as several honor missions that are integrated into the main chapter progression.

## Honor / Stranger Missions Included in Chapter Progression

The following missions are both Stranger missions and part of the chapter progression:

- Brothers and Sisters, One and All
- Do Not Seek Absolution
- Help a Brother Out
- Money Lending and Other Sins
- Of Men and Angels

## Important Notes

- Stranger missions may have chapter-specific availability.
- Some missions can become unavailable depending on story progression.
- Some Stranger missions contain multiple parts.
- Honor missions can affect the player's Honor-related progression.
- Completion of available Stranger missions contributes toward overall game completion.
