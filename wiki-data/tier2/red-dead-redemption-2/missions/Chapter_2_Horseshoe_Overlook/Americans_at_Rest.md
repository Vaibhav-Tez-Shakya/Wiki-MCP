# Americans at Rest

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2
**Tier:** 1
**Chapter:** Chapter 2 — Horseshoe Overlook
**Mission Type:** Main Story Mission

## Overview

Arthur meets Javier at a saloon in Valentine and becomes involved in a confrontation with local troublemakers.

## Objectives

- Meet Javier.
- Enter the saloon.
- Investigate the situation.
- Fight the hostile men.
- Escape the confrontation.

## Walkthrough

Arthur joins Javier at the Valentine saloon. A confrontation develops and Arthur becomes involved in a fistfight.

The mission establishes Arthur's relationship with Javier and introduces another aspect of life in Valentine.
