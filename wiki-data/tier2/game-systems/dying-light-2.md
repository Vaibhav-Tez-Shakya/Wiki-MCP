# Dying Light 2 — Game Systems

## Leveling and XP

Dying Light 2 does not use a conventional single character-level system for its main skill progression. XP is primarily divided between Combat and Parkour progression.

Combat XP contributes toward Combat skill progression.

Parkour XP contributes toward Parkour skill progression.

## Character Progression

Player progression is represented through:
- Combat skills
- Parkour skills
- Health
- Stamina
- Weapons
- Equipment
- Story progression
- World progression

## Combat Skill System

Combat XP is used to unlock Combat skills.

Combat skills provide additional attacks, defensive options, movement techniques, and combat capabilities.

## Parkour Skill System

Parkour XP is used to unlock Parkour skills.

Parkour skills improve movement, traversal, climbing, jumping, and navigation capabilities.

## Skills and Abilities

The two primary skill trees are:
- Combat
- Parkour

They progress independently and improve different aspects of gameplay.

## Equipment Progression

Weapons and equipment affect combat effectiveness.

Gear can provide different attributes and quality levels, making equipment selection an important part of progression.

## Combat Progression

Combat effectiveness depends on:
- Combat skills
- Weapons
- Equipment
- Health
- Stamina
- Enemy strength
- Player execution

## Difficulty Scaling

Difficulty is influenced by enemy strength, encounters, player progression, equipment, and selected difficulty settings.

It should not be represented only as a character-level comparison.

## Quest Progression

Main and side quests provide story progression, rewards, experience, equipment, and resources.

Quest progression also unlocks additional activities and parts of the world.

## World Progression

World progression includes:
- Story progression
- Area access
- Settlements
- Exploration
- Activities
- Facilities
- Parkour traversal

## Resource Systems

Important resources include:
- Combat XP
- Parkour XP
- Weapons
- Equipment
- Crafting materials
- Consumables
- Health
- Stamina

## Endgame Progression

Endgame progression can include additional activities, exploration, equipment collection, combat challenges, and other repeatable systems.
