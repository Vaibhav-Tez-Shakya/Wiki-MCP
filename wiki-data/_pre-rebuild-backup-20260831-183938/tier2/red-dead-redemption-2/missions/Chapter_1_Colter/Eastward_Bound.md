# Eastward Bound

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2  
**Tier:** 1  
**Chapter:** Chapter 1 — Colter  
**Mission Type:** Main Story Mission  
**Order:** 6  
**Location:** Colter to Horseshoe Overlook  
**Branch:** None

## Overview

The Van der Linde gang leaves the abandoned settlement of Colter after surviving the severe winter conditions. Dutch leads the gang south toward a new camp at Horseshoe Overlook.

## Objectives

- Leave Colter with the gang.
- Travel with the gang through the mountains.
- Follow the wagon convoy.
- Deal with obstacles encountered during the journey.
- Reach Horseshoe Overlook.

## Walkthrough

After the events at Colter, Dutch decides that the gang must leave the mountains and search for a safer place to establish camp.

Arthur travels with the gang as they move south through the snowy wilderness. The group encounters difficult terrain during the journey and eventually reaches the area surrounding Horseshoe Overlook.

The mission concludes the Colter chapter and transitions the story into Chapter 2.
