# Icarus and Friends

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2
**Tier:** 1
**Chapter:** Chapter 6 — Beaver Hollow
**Mission Type:** Main Story Mission
**Location:** Saint Denis
**Branch:** None

## Overview

Arthur and Sadie use a hot-air balloon to observe Saint Denis while gathering information.

## Objectives

- Meet Sadie.
- Reach the balloon.
- Fly over Saint Denis.
- Observe the area.
- Gather information.
- Return safely.

## Walkthrough

Arthur joins Sadie Adler and uses a hot-air balloon to survey the area.

The unusual vantage point provides information that helps the gang's ongoing operations.
