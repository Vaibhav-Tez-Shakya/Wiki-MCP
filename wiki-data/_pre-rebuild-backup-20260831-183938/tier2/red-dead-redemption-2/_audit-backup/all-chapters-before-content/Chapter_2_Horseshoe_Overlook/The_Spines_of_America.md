# The Spines of America

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2  
**Tier:** 1  
**Chapter:** Chapter 2 — Horseshoe Overlook  
**Mission Type:** Main Story Mission  
**Location:** Chez Porter and Valentine  
**Order:** Chapter 2

## Overview

Arthur and Hosea plan and carry out a wagon robbery after deciding to acquire a valuable wagon.

## Objectives

- Meet Hosea.
- Travel to the target location.
- Observe the wagon.
- Steal the wagon.
- Escape the area.
- Deliver the wagon.

## Walkthrough

Arthur joins Hosea for a wagon theft. They approach the target carefully and take the wagon before escaping the area.

The wagon is delivered to the appropriate location, providing the gang with money and resources.

## Characters

- Arthur Morgan
- Hosea Matthews

## Location

Chez Porter and surrounding roads.

## Rewards

Money and wagon-related progression.

## Mission Outcome

Arthur and Hosea successfully steal the wagon and complete the operation.

## Branch Relationship

Main Chapter 2 mission.
