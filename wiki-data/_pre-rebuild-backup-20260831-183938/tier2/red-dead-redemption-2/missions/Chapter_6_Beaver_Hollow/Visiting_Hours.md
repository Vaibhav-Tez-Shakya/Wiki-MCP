# Visiting Hours

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2
**Tier:** 1
**Chapter:** Chapter 6 — Beaver Hollow
**Mission Type:** Main Story Mission
**Location:** Sisika Penitentiary
**Branch:** None

## Overview

Arthur and Sadie travel to Sisika Penitentiary to rescue John Marston.

## Objectives

- Meet Sadie.
- Travel to Sisika.
- Infiltrate the prison.
- Locate John.
- Rescue John.
- Escape.

## Walkthrough

Arthur and Sadie plan and execute a rescue mission at Sisika Penitentiary.

They infiltrate the prison, locate John and fight their way out.

## Characters

- Sadie Adler
- John Marston
- Prison guards

## Location

Sisika Penitentiary.

## Rewards

Story progression and John's rescue.

## Mission Outcome

John is rescued from prison and returned to the gang.
