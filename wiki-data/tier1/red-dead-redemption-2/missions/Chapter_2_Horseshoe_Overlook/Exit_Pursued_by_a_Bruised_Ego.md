# Exit, Pursued by a Bruised Ego

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2
**Tier:** 1
**Chapter:** Chapter 2 — Horseshoe Overlook
**Mission Type:** Main Story Mission
**Order:** 1
**Location:** Valentine and surrounding wilderness
**Branch:** None

## Overview

Arthur helps Hosea track a dangerous bear while learning the game's hunting and tracking systems.

## Objectives

- Ride with Hosea.
- Track the bear.
- Search for clues.
- Hunt the bear.
- Return toward camp.

## Walkthrough

Arthur accompanies Hosea into the wilderness to hunt a large bear. The mission introduces advanced tracking and hunting mechanics.

After locating the bear, Arthur and Hosea confront the animal before returning from the hunting expedition.

## Characters

- Arthur Morgan
- Hosea Matthews

## Location

Wilderness near Horseshoe Overlook.

## Rewards

Hunting experience, story progression and access to additional hunting opportunities.

## Mission Outcome

Arthur and Hosea complete their bear hunt and strengthen their hunting partnership.

## Branch Relationship

This mission begins the Chapter 2 mission progression.
