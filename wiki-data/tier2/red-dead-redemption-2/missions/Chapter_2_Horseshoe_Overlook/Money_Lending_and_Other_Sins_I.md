# Money Lending and Other Sins I

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2
**Tier:** 1
**Chapter:** Chapter 2 — Horseshoe Overlook
**Mission Type:** Camp Mission
**Location:** Horseshoe Overlook
**Branch:** None

## Overview

Arthur begins collecting debts for Leopold Strauss as part of the gang's lending operation.

## Objectives

- Speak with Strauss.
- Locate the debtor.
- Collect the debt.
- Return to camp.

## Walkthrough

Leopold Strauss assigns Arthur debt-collection work. Arthur travels to the debtor and attempts to recover the money owed to the gang.

The mission introduces the gang's money-lending activities.

## Characters

- Arthur Morgan
- Leopold Strauss

## Location

Horseshoe Overlook and surrounding area.

## Rewards

Money and contribution to the camp ledger.

## Mission Outcome

Arthur begins working as Strauss's debt collector.

## Branch Relationship

Beginning of the Money Lending and Other Sins mission chain.
