# Dragon's Dogma 2 — Game Systems

## Leveling and XP

Dragon's Dogma 2 uses a traditional character-level progression system. Experience is gained through combat, quests, exploration, and defeating enemies. Increasing character level improves overall character attributes and progression.

## Character Progression

Character progression is determined by:
- Character level
- Vocation
- Vocation rank
- Skills
- Core abilities
- Equipment
- Character attributes

## Vocation System

The vocation system determines the player's combat role and available abilities. Different vocations provide different weapons, skills, core abilities, and combat approaches.

The Arisen can change vocations during the game, allowing different progression and play styles.

## Skills and Abilities

Each vocation provides vocation-specific skills and core abilities. Skill progression expands the player's combat options and effectiveness.

## Equipment Progression

Weapons and armor are major components of character power. Equipment can be replaced and upgraded as the player progresses.

Equipment strength should be considered alongside character level and vocation progression.

## Combat Progression

Combat effectiveness depends on:
- Character level
- Vocation
- Vocation skills
- Equipment
- Party composition
- Enemy type
- Player execution

## Difficulty Scaling

Dragon's Dogma 2 does not rely exclusively on selectable difficulty levels. Enemy difficulty varies by enemy type, location, encounter, and progression.

Exploration can expose players to substantially stronger enemies.

## Quest Progression

Quests provide story progression, rewards, exploration opportunities, and character progression.

Quest difficulty is influenced by location, enemy encounters, equipment, vocation, and player preparation.

## World Progression

World progression is strongly connected to exploration, story quests, locations, NPC interactions, and discovery.

## Resource Systems

Important resources include:
- Gold
- Experience
- Health
- Stamina
- Weapons
- Armor
- Consumables
- Upgrade materials

## Endgame Progression

Endgame progression continues through exploration, stronger encounters, equipment improvement, vocation development, and additional activities.
