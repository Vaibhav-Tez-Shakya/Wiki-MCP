# An Honest Mistake

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2
**Tier:** 1
**Chapter:** Chapter 3 — Clemens Point
**Mission Type:** Main Story Mission
**Location:** Scarlett Meadows
**Branch:** None

## Overview

Arthur and the gang attempt a robbery that quickly turns into a dangerous escape.

## Objectives

- Travel with Hosea.
- Prepare the robbery.
- Enter the target location.
- Escape the ambush.
- Survive the pursuit.

## Walkthrough

Arthur joins Hosea and other gang members for a robbery. The plan goes wrong and the group must fight its way out.

The mission emphasizes the increasing danger surrounding the gang's criminal activities.
