# Pouring Forth Oil I

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2  
**Tier:** 1  
**Chapter:** Chapter 2 — Horseshoe Overlook  
**Mission Type:** Camp Mission  
**Location:** Valentine and surrounding area  
**Order:** Chapter 2

## Overview

Arthur helps John prepare a plan to rob an oil wagon as part of a train robbery scheme.

## Objectives

- Meet John.
- Locate the oil wagon.
- Observe the wagon route.
- Prepare for the robbery.

## Walkthrough

Arthur meets John Marston, who explains the plan to steal an oil wagon. The wagon will later be used as part of an ambush against a train.

Arthur assists with the preparation for the robbery.

## Characters

- Arthur Morgan
- John Marston

## Location

Valentine and surrounding roads.

## Rewards

Preparation for the Pouring Forth Oil mission chain.

## Mission Outcome

Arthur and John establish the plan for obtaining an oil wagon.

## Branch Relationship

First part of the Pouring Forth Oil mission chain.
