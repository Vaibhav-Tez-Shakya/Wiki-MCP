# Fleeting Joy

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2
**Tier:** 1
**Chapter:** Chapter 5 — Guarma
**Mission Type:** Main Story Mission
**Location:** Lakay
**Branch:** None

## Overview

The gang comes under attack shortly after returning from Guarma.

## Objectives

- Defend Lakay.
- Fight the attackers.
- Protect the gang.
- Escape the attack.

## Walkthrough

The gang attempts to recover at Lakay, but the hideout is attacked by Pinkerton agents.

Arthur and the others fight back and attempt to survive the assault.

Story progression.

The gang survives the attack but is forced to reconsider its position.
