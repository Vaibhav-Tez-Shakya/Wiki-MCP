# Dear Uncle Tacitus

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2
**Tier:** 1
**Chapter:** Chapter 5 — Guarma
**Mission Type:** Main Story Mission
**Location:** Lemoyne
**Branch:** None

## Overview

Arthur returns from Guarma and attempts to reconnect with the gang at Lakay.

## Objectives

- Return to the mainland.
- Travel toward the gang.
- Search for the camp.
- Find the gang members.

## Walkthrough

After returning from Guarma, Arthur travels through Lemoyne while searching for the gang.

He eventually locates the relocated camp and reconnects with the surviving members.

## Characters

- Arthur Morgan
- Dutch van der Linde
- Abigail Roberts
- Gang members

## Location

Lemoyne and Lakay.

## Rewards

Story progression.

## Mission Outcome

Arthur reunites with the gang after returning from Guarma.

## Branch Relationship
