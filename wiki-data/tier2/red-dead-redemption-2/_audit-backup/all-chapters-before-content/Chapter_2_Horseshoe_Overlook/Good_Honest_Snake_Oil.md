# Good, Honest, Snake Oil

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2  
**Tier:** 1  
**Chapter:** Chapter 2 — Horseshoe Overlook  
**Mission Type:** Stranger Mission  
**Location:** Valentine  
**Order:** Chapter 2

## Overview

Arthur helps a lawman track down a fraudulent snake-oil salesman wanted for his crimes.

## Objectives

- Speak with the sheriff.
- Accept the bounty.
- Locate Benedict Allbright.
- Track him to the river.
- Capture him.
- Return him to the sheriff.

## Walkthrough

Arthur accepts a bounty from the Valentine sheriff and searches for Benedict Allbright, a traveling salesman accused of poisoning people with fraudulent medicine.

Arthur finds Allbright near the river, captures him, and transports him back to Valentine.

## Characters

- Arthur Morgan
- Benedict Allbright
- Valentine sheriff

## Location

Valentine and Dakota River.

## Rewards

Bounty payment and story progression.

## Mission Outcome

Arthur captures Benedict Allbright and delivers him to the authorities.

## Branch Relationship

Optional bounty mission available during Chapter 2.
