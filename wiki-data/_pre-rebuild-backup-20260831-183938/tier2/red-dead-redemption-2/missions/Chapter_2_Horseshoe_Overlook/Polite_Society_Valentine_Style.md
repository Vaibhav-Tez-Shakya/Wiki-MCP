# Polite Society, Valentine Style

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2
**Tier:** 1
**Chapter:** Chapter 2 — Horseshoe Overlook
**Mission Type:** Main Story Mission
**Location:** Valentine
**Branch:** None

## Overview

Arthur accompanies several members of the gang into Valentine while dealing with local trouble and learning more about the town.

## Objectives

- Ride to Valentine.
- Search for the missing women.
- Investigate the town.
- Deal with hostile locals.
- Return to camp.

## Walkthrough

Arthur travels to Valentine with members of the gang. What begins as a simple visit develops into a series of incidents involving locals and law enforcement.

The mission establishes Valentine as an important location during Chapter 2.
