# The Spines of America

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2
**Tier:** 1
**Chapter:** Chapter 2 — Horseshoe Overlook
**Mission Type:** Main Story Mission
**Location:** Valentine and Carmody Dell
**Branch:** None

## Overview

Arthur and Hosea plan and execute a stagecoach robbery while targeting a coach near Valentine.

## Objectives

- Meet Hosea.
- Travel to Carmody Dell.
- Scout the location.
- Steal the stagecoach.
- Escape with the coach.
- Deliver it to the buyer.

## Walkthrough

Arthur works with Hosea on a stagecoach robbery. They approach the target, steal the coach and transport it away from the area.

The mission introduces the stagecoach robbery system and expands Arthur's criminal activities around Valentine.

## Characters

- Arthur Morgan
- Hosea Matthews

## Location

Carmody Dell and Valentine region.

## Rewards

Money from the stagecoach robbery and story progression.

## Mission Outcome

Arthur and Hosea successfully complete the robbery.

## Branch Relationship

Standalone Chapter 2 mission.
