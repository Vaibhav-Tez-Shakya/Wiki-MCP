## Timing Notes

- The best general time to trigger companion item requests is from noon into the evening.
- Lenny's request is available at night and is best attempted around 10PM.
- Some requests are tied to specific missions or companion activities.
- Certain requests may become unavailable as the story progresses.
- Completing requests early is recommended when possible.

## Availability Notes

Some companion requests have special conditions:

- Pearson's request can occur while playing poker.
- Jack's first request occurs during A Fisher of Men.
- Jack's second request occurs after A Fisher of Men.
- Abigail's request occurs after A Fisher of Men.
- Tilly's request requires playing dominoes.
- Sadie's request is connected to Further Questions of Female Suffrage.
- Lenny's request occurs during nighttime hours.
- Some Chapter 3-4 requests may not always trigger immediately.
