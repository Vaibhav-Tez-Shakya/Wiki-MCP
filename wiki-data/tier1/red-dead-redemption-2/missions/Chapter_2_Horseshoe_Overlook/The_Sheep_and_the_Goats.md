# The Sheep and the Goats

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2
**Tier:** 1
**Chapter:** Chapter 2 — Horseshoe Overlook
**Mission Type:** Main Story Mission
**Location:** Valentine
**Branch:** None

## Overview

Arthur helps John move a herd of sheep while attempting to make money through a risky livestock deal.

## Objectives

- Meet John in Valentine.
- Obtain the sheep.
- Move the herd.
- Drive the sheep toward Valentine.
- Deal with the resulting confrontation.

## Walkthrough

Arthur joins John Marston and helps acquire a herd of sheep. The animals are moved toward Valentine as part of a plan to make money.

The operation becomes complicated when lawmen and other forces become involved.

## Characters

- Arthur Morgan
- John Marston
- Valentine lawmen
- Local livestock traders

## Location

Valentine and surrounding countryside.

## Rewards

Money and story progression.

## Mission Outcome

The sheep operation leads directly into a major confrontation in Valentine.

## Branch Relationship

Standalone Chapter 2 mission.
