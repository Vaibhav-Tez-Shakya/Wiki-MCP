# Paying a Social Call

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2
**Tier:** 1
**Chapter:** Chapter 2 — Horseshoe Overlook
**Mission Type:** Main Story Mission
**Location:** Six Point Cabin
**Branch:** None

## Overview

Arthur, Bill and other members of the gang travel to an O'Driscoll hideout to confront the rival gang.

## Objectives

- Travel to the O'Driscoll hideout.
- Scout the cabin.
- Attack the O'Driscolls.
- Search the cabin.
- Escape the area.

## Walkthrough

Arthur joins members of the Van der Linde gang in an assault on an O'Driscoll hideout.

The attack reinforces the ongoing feud between the two gangs.

## Characters

- Arthur Morgan
- Bill Williamson
- O'Driscoll gang members

## Location

Six Point Cabin.

## Rewards

Loot and story progression.

## Mission Outcome

The gang attacks the O'Driscoll hideout and continues its conflict with Colm O'Driscoll.

## Branch Relationship

Standalone Chapter 2 mission.
