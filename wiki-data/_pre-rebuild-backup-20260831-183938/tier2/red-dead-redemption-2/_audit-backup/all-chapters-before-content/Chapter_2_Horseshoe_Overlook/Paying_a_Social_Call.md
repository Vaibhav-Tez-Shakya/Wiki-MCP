# Paying a Social Call

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2  
**Tier:** 1  
**Chapter:** Chapter 2 — Horseshoe Overlook  
**Mission Type:** Main Story Mission  
**Location:** Chez Porter area  
**Order:** Chapter 2

## Overview

Arthur, Javier, and Charles visit an O'Driscoll hideout while searching for information.

## Objectives

- Ride with Javier and Charles.
- Locate the hideout.
- Approach the O'Driscolls.
- Attack the camp.
- Search the area.
- Return to camp.

## Walkthrough

Arthur rides with Javier and Charles to investigate an O'Driscoll location. The group attacks the hideout and searches it after defeating the occupants.

The mission continues the gang's ongoing conflict with the O'Driscolls.

## Characters

- Arthur Morgan
- Javier Escuella
- Charles Smith
- O'Driscoll gang members

## Location

O'Driscoll hideout near Chez Porter.

## Rewards

Loot, supplies, and story progression.

## Mission Outcome

Arthur and his companions attack the O'Driscoll hideout and recover useful information and supplies.

Main Chapter 2 mission.
