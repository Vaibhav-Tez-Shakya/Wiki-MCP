# Enter, Pursued by a Memory

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2  
**Tier:** 1  
**Chapter:** Chapter 1 — Colter  
**Mission Type:** Main Story Mission  
**Order:** 2  
**Location:** Colter and surrounding mountains  
**Branch:** None

## Overview

Arthur Morgan joins Javier Escuella in a search for John Marston, who has been separated from the gang and is seriously injured after an encounter with wolves.

## Objectives

- Travel with Javier into the mountains.
- Search for John Marston.
- Follow John's trail.
- Locate John.
- Defend John from the wolves.
- Return toward camp with John.

## Walkthrough

Arthur and Javier leave Colter to search the surrounding mountains for John. They follow John's trail through the snow until they locate him badly injured.

A group of wolves attacks while Arthur and Javier attempt to rescue John. After dealing with the wolves, Arthur and Javier escort John back toward the gang's temporary refuge.

## Characters

- Javier Escuella
- John Marston

The mountains surrounding Colter

John is rescued and returned toward the gang's camp, although his injuries remain serious.
