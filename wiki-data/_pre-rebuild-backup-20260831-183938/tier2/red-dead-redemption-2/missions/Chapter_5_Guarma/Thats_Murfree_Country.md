# That's Murfree Country

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2
**Tier:** 1
**Chapter:** Chapter 5 — Guarma
**Mission Type:** Main Story Mission
**Location:** Roanoke Ridge
**Branch:** None

## Overview

Arthur and Charles investigate a dangerous region controlled by the Murfree Brood.

## Objectives

- Travel with Charles.
- Investigate the area.
- Locate the Murfree Brood.
- Rescue civilians.
- Clear the threat.

## Walkthrough

Arthur and Charles travel through Roanoke Ridge while investigating reports of violence.

They encounter the Murfree Brood and rescue survivors from the group's attacks.

## Characters

- Arthur Morgan
- Charles Smith
- Murfree Brood members

## Location

Roanoke Ridge.

## Rewards

Story progression and information about a potential new camp location.

## Mission Outcome

Arthur and Charles investigate the Murfree Brood and prepare for the gang's eventual relocation.
