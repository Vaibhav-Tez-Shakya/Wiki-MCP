# A Strange Kindness

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2
**Tier:** 1
**Chapter:** Chapter 2 — Horseshoe Overlook
**Mission Type:** Main Story Mission
**Location:** Dewberry Creek
**Branch:** None

## Overview

Arthur, Charles and John search for a missing gang member and discover an abandoned homestead containing evidence of criminal activity.

## Objectives

- Search the area.
- Investigate the homestead.
- Rescue the kidnapped person.
- Escape the attackers.
- Return safely to camp.

## Walkthrough

Arthur and other members of the gang investigate a suspicious homestead while searching for information. They discover that people are being held captive and intervene.

The mission combines investigation, combat and rescue before Arthur returns to camp.

## Characters

- Arthur Morgan
- Charles Smith
- John Marston
- Hostile gang members

## Location

Dewberry Creek.

## Rewards

Story progression and improved camp access.

## Mission Outcome

Arthur and the gang rescue the captive and return safely.

## Branch Relationship

Standalone Chapter 2 mission.
