## Overview

Arthur accompanies Uncle, Karen, and Mary-Beth into Valentine while the group explores the town.

## Objectives

- Travel to Valentine.
- Follow Uncle and the women.
- Explore the town.
- Deal with the horse theft incident.
- Escape the authorities.

## Walkthrough

Arthur travels to Valentine with members of the gang. The outing quickly becomes complicated when the group becomes involved in a horse theft.

Arthur assists his companions and deals with the consequences before returning toward camp.
