# The First Shall Be Last

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2
**Tier:** 1
**Chapter:** Chapter 2 — Horseshoe Overlook
**Mission Type:** Main Story Mission
**Location:** Scarlett Meadows region
**Branch:** None

## Overview

Arthur joins Javier and Charles in rescuing Sean MacGuire after he is captured by bounty hunters.

## Objectives

- Meet Javier and Charles.
- Locate Sean.
- Scout the enemy camp.
- Rescue Sean.
- Escape with the group.

## Walkthrough

Arthur travels with Javier Escuella and Charles Smith to locate Sean MacGuire.

After finding Sean held by bounty hunters, Arthur and the group launch a rescue operation.

## Characters

- Arthur Morgan
- Javier Escuella
- Charles Smith
- Sean MacGuire
- Bounty hunters

## Location

Scarlett Meadows.

## Rewards

Story progression and improved gang relations.

## Mission Outcome

Sean is rescued and returned to the gang.
