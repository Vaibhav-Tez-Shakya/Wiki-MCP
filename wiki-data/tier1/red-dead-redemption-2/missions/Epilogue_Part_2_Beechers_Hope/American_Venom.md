# American Venom

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2
**Tier:** 1
**Chapter:** Epilogue Part 2 — Beecher's Hope
**Mission Type:** Epilogue Mission
**Location:** Mount Hagen
**Branch:** Final mission

## Overview

John, Sadie and Charles track down Micah Bell for a final confrontation.

## Objectives

- Meet Sadie and Charles.
- Track Micah.
- Travel to Mount Hagen.
- Fight Micah's gang.
- Confront Micah.
- Resolve the final confrontation.

## Walkthrough

John joins Sadie Adler and Charles Smith in tracking Micah Bell.

The group travels through the mountains and attacks Micah's hideout. John confronts Micah in the final major conflict of the story.
