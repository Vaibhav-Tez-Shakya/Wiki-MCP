# Dying Light 2 — Game Systems

## Leveling and XP

Dying Light 2 uses multiple progression systems focused on combat, parkour, equipment, and character development.

- Gameplay activities contribute to progression.
- Combat and parkour progression are separate dimensions.
- Combat activities contribute toward combat development.
- Parkour activities contribute toward parkour development.
- Character progression should not be represented by a single RPG-style level alone.

## Character Progression

Character effectiveness depends on:

- Combat skills
- Parkour skills
- Weapons

- Stamina
- Player execution

## Combat Skill System

Combat progression develops abilities related to:

- Melee combat
- Ranged combat
- Dodging
- Blocking
- Enemy interaction
- Combat mobility

Combat skills allow different combat approaches.

## Parkour Skill System

Parkour progression improves:

- Movement
- Jumping
- Climbing
- Traversal
- Air movement
- Mobility

Parkour progression affects both exploration and combat mobility.

## Skills and Abilities

The major skill categories are:

- Combat
- Parkour

Players can develop different combinations of combat and movement abilities.

## Equipment Progression

Equipment includes:

- Weapons
- Gear

- Consumables

Equipment and gear bonuses can influence combat effectiveness and build specialization.

## Cross-Game Comparison Policy

Dying Light 2 should not be assigned an artificial equivalent character level from another game.

Comparison should use:

- Combat progression
- Parkour progression
- Equipment
- Enemy strength
- Difficulty
- Quest progression
- World progression

## Numerical Data Policy

Numerical progression values must be explicitly documented before being treated as factual.
