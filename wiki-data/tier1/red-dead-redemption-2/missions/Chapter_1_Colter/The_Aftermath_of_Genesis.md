# The Aftermath of Genesis

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2  
**Tier:** 1  
**Chapter:** Chapter 1 — Colter  
**Mission Type:** Main Story Mission  
**Order:** 3  
**Location:** Colter and surrounding wilderness  
**Branch:** Main Story

## Overview

Arthur and Charles leave Colter to hunt for food while the Van der Linde gang struggles to survive the harsh winter.

## Objectives

- Travel with Charles.
- Follow the trail into the wilderness.
- Track a deer.
- Hunt the animal.
- Collect the meat.
- Return to camp with the food.

## Walkthrough

Arthur accompanies Charles into the snowy wilderness surrounding Colter to search for food.

Charles teaches Arthur how to track animals through the snow. Arthur follows the trail and hunts a deer, providing meat that can be brought back to the gang.

The mission introduces the hunting and tracking mechanics while showing the gang's immediate need for food and supplies.

## Characters

- Arthur Morgan
- Charles Smith

## Location

The wilderness surrounding Colter.

## Rewards

Food for the gang and story progression.

## Mission Outcome

Arthur and Charles successfully obtain food and return toward the gang's camp, helping the group survive the harsh conditions at Colter.

## Branch Relationship

This mission forms part of the Chapter 1 main-story progression.
