# The Course of True Love I

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2
**Tier:** 1
**Chapter:** Chapter 3 — Clemens Point
**Mission Type:** Stranger Mission
**Location:** Rhodes
**Branch:** Part of The Course of True Love chain

## Overview

Arthur becomes involved in the secret relationship between Beau Gray and Penelope Braithwaite.

## Objectives

- Meet Beau Gray.
- Learn about the relationship.
- Deliver messages or items.
- Assist Beau.

## Walkthrough

Arthur encounters Beau Gray and learns about his relationship with Penelope Braithwaite.

The mission introduces the conflict surrounding their families and begins Arthur's involvement in their story.

- Beau Gray
- Penelope Braithwaite

Rhodes and surrounding plantations.

## Rewards

Story progression.

Arthur agrees to assist Beau and Penelope.
