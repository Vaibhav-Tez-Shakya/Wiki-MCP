**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2

## Overview

Arthur searches Saint Denis for information about Angelo Bronte while helping Dutch and the gang establish themselves in the city.

- Search Saint Denis.
- Find information about Jack.

Arthur explores Saint Denis while searching for information connected to Jack Marston and the criminal underworld.

The mission introduces the gang to the city's dense urban environment.
