# A Strange Kindness

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2  
**Tier:** 1  
**Chapter:** Chapter 2 — Horseshoe Overlook  
**Mission Type:** Main Story Mission  
**Location:** Six Point Cabin and surrounding wilderness  
**Order:** Chapter 2

## Overview

Arthur and Dutch investigate a possible camp location after learning that a family has been living in suspicious circumstances.

## Objectives

- Travel with Dutch.
- Investigate the area.
- Approach the cabin.
- Confront the men occupying the location.
- Rescue the captive.
- Escape the area.

## Walkthrough

Arthur accompanies Dutch while searching for a safer place for the gang to establish itself. They discover a cabin occupied by hostile men and find evidence that someone is being held captive.

Arthur and Dutch attack the men, rescue the captive, and leave the area.

## Characters

- Arthur Morgan
- Dutch van der Linde
- Captive
- Hostile outlaws

## Location

Six Point Cabin and surrounding wilderness.

## Rewards

Story progression and supplies.

## Mission Outcome

Arthur and Dutch secure information about a possible camp location and rescue a captive.
