# Pouring Forth Oil III

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2  
**Tier:** 1  
**Chapter:** Chapter 2 — Horseshoe Overlook  
**Mission Type:** Camp Mission  
**Location:** Heartlands  
**Order:** Chapter 2

## Overview

Arthur completes another stage of John's preparation for the train robbery.

## Objectives

- Meet John.
- Prepare the stolen oil wagon.
- Move the wagon into position.
- Coordinate with the gang.

## Walkthrough

Arthur helps John prepare the equipment required for the planned train robbery. The oil wagon is positioned so it can be used to stop the train.

## Characters

- Arthur Morgan
- John Marston
- Van der Linde gang members

## Location

Heartlands and railway route.

## Rewards

Progress toward the train robbery and story progression.

## Mission Outcome

The gang completes another stage of preparation for the train robbery.

## Branch Relationship

Third part of the Pouring Forth Oil mission chain.
