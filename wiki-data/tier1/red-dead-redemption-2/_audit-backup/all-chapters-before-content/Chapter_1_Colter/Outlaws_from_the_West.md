# Outlaws from the West

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2  
**Tier:** 1  
**Chapter:** Chapter 1 — Colter  
**Mission Type:** Main Story Mission  
**Order:** 1  
**Location:** Colter  
**Branch:** None

## Overview

Arthur Morgan and the Van der Linde gang reach the abandoned settlement of Colter while fleeing Blackwater and searching for shelter in the mountains.

## Objectives

- Reach the settlement of Colter with the gang.
- Search the abandoned buildings for survivors and supplies.
- Follow Dutch and the gang as they secure the area.
- Prepare Colter as a temporary camp.

## Walkthrough

The mission introduces the player to the gang's situation after the failed Blackwater robbery. Arthur travels with Dutch and the others through the snow toward Colter.

After reaching the settlement, the gang searches the abandoned buildings and establishes a temporary base while attempting to determine what happened to the inhabitants.
