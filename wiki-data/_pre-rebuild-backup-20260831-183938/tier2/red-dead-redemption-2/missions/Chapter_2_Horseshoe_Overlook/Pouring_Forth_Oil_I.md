# Pouring Forth Oil I

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2
**Tier:** 1
**Chapter:** Chapter 2 — Horseshoe Overlook
**Mission Type:** Camp Mission
**Location:** Valentine region
**Branch:** Part of the Pouring Forth Oil chain

## Overview

Arthur begins a plan with John to rob a train using an oil wagon.

## Objectives

- Meet John.
- Discuss the train robbery.
- Locate the oil wagon.
- Prepare the robbery plan.

## Walkthrough

Arthur works with John Marston to prepare a train robbery. The gang needs an oil wagon as part of the plan.

The mission establishes the preparation phase of the robbery.
