# Pouring Forth Oil IV

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2  
**Tier:** 1  
**Chapter:** Chapter 2 — Horseshoe Overlook  
**Mission Type:** Main Story Mission  
**Location:** Heartlands railway  
**Order:** Chapter 2

## Overview

Arthur and the gang use the stolen oil wagon to ambush and rob a train.

## Objectives

- Meet John and the gang.
- Position the oil wagon on the railway.
- Stop the train.
- Board the train.
- Fight the guards.
- Loot the train.
- Escape.

## Walkthrough

Arthur joins John and the gang for the planned train robbery. The oil wagon is used to stop the train, allowing the gang to board it.

Arthur fights the guards and helps the gang seize valuables before escaping.

## Characters

- Arthur Morgan
- John Marston
- Charles Smith
- Sean MacGuire
- Lenny Summers
- Bill Williamson
- Train guards

## Location

Railway in the Heartlands.

## Rewards

Money, valuables, supplies, and story progression.

## Mission Outcome

The gang successfully robs the train and escapes with the proceeds.

## Branch Relationship

Final part of the Pouring Forth Oil mission chain.
