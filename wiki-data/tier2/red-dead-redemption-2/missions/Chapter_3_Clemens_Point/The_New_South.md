# The New South

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2
**Tier:** 1
**Chapter:** Chapter 3 — Clemens Point
**Mission Type:** Main Story Mission
**Location:** Rhodes and surrounding area
**Branch:** None

## Overview

Arthur accompanies Dutch and Hosea to Rhodes as the gang begins manipulating the feud between the Gray and Braithwaite families.

## Objectives

- Travel to Rhodes.
- Assist the lawmen.
- Deal with the prisoners.
- Return to camp.

## Walkthrough

Arthur rides with Dutch and Hosea toward Rhodes. The gang begins establishing relationships with local authorities while positioning itself between the Gray and Braithwaite families.

## Characters

- Arthur Morgan
- Dutch van der Linde
- Hosea Matthews
- Sheriff Gray

## Location

Rhodes.

## Rewards

Story progression and access to Rhodes activities.

## Mission Outcome

The gang establishes itself in Rhodes and begins exploiting the local family feud.

## Branch Relationship

Beginning of the main Chapter 3 storyline.
