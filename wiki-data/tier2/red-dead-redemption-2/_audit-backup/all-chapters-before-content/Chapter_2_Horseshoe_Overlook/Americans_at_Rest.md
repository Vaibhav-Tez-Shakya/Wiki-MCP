# Americans at Rest

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2  
**Tier:** 1  
**Chapter:** Chapter 2 — Horseshoe Overlook  
**Mission Type:** Main Story Mission  
**Location:** Valentine saloon  
**Order:** Chapter 2

## Overview

Arthur joins Javier and Charles at a saloon in Valentine, where a confrontation quickly turns into a violent brawl.

## Objectives

- Meet Javier and Charles.
- Enter the saloon.
- Participate in the fight.
- Defeat the attackers.
- Leave the saloon.

## Walkthrough

Arthur joins Javier and Charles at the Valentine saloon. A disagreement escalates into a fistfight, forcing Arthur to defend himself and his companions.

After the fight, Arthur and the others leave the saloon.

## Characters

- Arthur Morgan
- Javier Escuella
- Charles Smith
- Valentine saloon patrons

## Location

Valentine saloon.

## Rewards

Story progression and increased familiarity with Valentine.

## Mission Outcome

Arthur, Javier, and Charles survive the saloon brawl.
