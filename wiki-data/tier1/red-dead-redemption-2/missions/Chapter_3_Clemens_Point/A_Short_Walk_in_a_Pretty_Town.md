# A Short Walk in a Pretty Town

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2
**Tier:** 1
**Chapter:** Chapter 3 — Clemens Point
**Mission Type:** Main Story Mission
**Location:** Rhodes
**Branch:** None

## Overview

Arthur and Bill meet Micah in Rhodes, where the gang becomes caught in escalating conflict involving the Gray family.

## Objectives

- Meet Bill and Micah.
- Enter Rhodes.
- Investigate the situation.
- Survive the ambush.
- Escape the town.

## Walkthrough

Arthur accompanies Bill Williamson and Micah Bell into Rhodes. The meeting becomes a violent confrontation and reveals that the gang has been manipulated by the local families.
