# Who Is Not Without Sin

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2
**Tier:** 1
**Chapter:** Chapter 2 — Horseshoe Overlook
**Mission Type:** Main Story Mission
**Location:** Flatneck Station and surrounding area
**Branch:** None

## Overview

Arthur searches for Reverend Swanson after he disappears while gambling and drinking.

## Objectives

- Locate Reverend Swanson.
- Search Flatneck Station.
- Find Swanson.
- Deal with his problems.
- Return him to camp.

## Walkthrough

Arthur travels to Flatneck Station looking for Reverend Swanson. He finds Swanson involved in a series of problems and helps him escape the situation.

Arthur then returns Swanson toward camp.

## Characters

- Arthur Morgan
- Reverend Swanson
- Gambling hall patrons

## Location

Flatneck Station.

## Rewards

Story progression and increased camp access.

## Mission Outcome

Arthur recovers Swanson and brings him back toward the gang.

## Branch Relationship

Standalone Chapter 2 mission.
