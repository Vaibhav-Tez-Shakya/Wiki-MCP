# The Sheep and the Goats

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2  
**Tier:** 1  
**Chapter:** Chapter 2 — Horseshoe Overlook  
**Mission Type:** Main Story Mission  
**Location:** Valentine and Heartlands  
**Order:** Chapter 2

## Overview

Arthur helps John obtain a herd of sheep as part of a plan to sell them in Valentine.

## Objectives

- Meet John.
- Travel to the sheep area.
- Herd the sheep.
- Drive them toward Valentine.
- Sell or deliver the sheep.
- Deal with the confrontation that follows.

## Walkthrough

Arthur and John travel into the Heartlands to acquire sheep. Arthur uses his horse and herding skills to move the animals toward Valentine.

The operation becomes complicated when the gang encounters local authorities and other armed men.

## Characters

- Arthur Morgan
- John Marston
- Valentine residents
- Local lawmen

## Location

Heartlands and Valentine.

## Rewards

Money, supplies, and story progression.

## Mission Outcome

Arthur and John complete the sheep operation, leading directly into the conflict that follows in Valentine.

## Branch Relationship

Main Chapter 2 mission.
