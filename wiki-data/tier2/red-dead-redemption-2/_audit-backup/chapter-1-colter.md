# Red Dead Redemption 2 — Chapter 1: Colter

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2
**Chapter:** 1
**Location:** Colter

After a failed robbery, the gang flee from Blackwater into the Grizzly Mountains. A late spring storm leaves them freezing and starving, so they shelter in an abandoned mining town while waiting for the weather to improve.
