# Pouring Forth Oil IV

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2
**Tier:** 1
**Chapter:** Chapter 2 — Horseshoe Overlook
**Mission Type:** Main Story Mission
**Location:** Railway near Valentine
**Branch:** Part of the Pouring Forth Oil chain

## Overview

Arthur and John execute the planned train robbery using the stolen oil wagon.

## Objectives

- Travel to the ambush location.
- Stop the train.
- Board the train.
- Fight the guards.
- Search the train.
- Escape with the gang.

## Walkthrough

Arthur and John use the oil wagon to stop a train. The gang boards the train and fights its guards while searching for valuables.

The robbery demonstrates the gang's coordinated criminal operations.

Railway near Valentine.

Money, valuables and story progression.

The gang successfully completes the train robbery and escapes.
