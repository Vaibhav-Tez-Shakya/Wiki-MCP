# Jim Milton Rides, Again?

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2  
**Tier:** 1  
**Chapter:** Epilogue Part 1 — Pronghorn Ranch  
**Mission Type:** Epilogue Mission  
**Location:** Pronghorn Ranch  
**Branch:** Main Story

## Overview

John Marston attempts to build a peaceful life at Pronghorn Ranch under the name Jim Milton, but his past as an outlaw quickly becomes relevant when the ranch is threatened.

## Objectives

- Work at Pronghorn Ranch.
- Protect the ranch.
- Respond to the threat against the ranch.
- Confront the attackers.
- Defend the ranch and its people.
- Help secure the property.

## Walkthrough

John continues living under the alias Jim Milton while working at Pronghorn Ranch. He attempts to keep his outlaw past hidden and establish a stable life for his family.

When the ranch comes under threat, John is forced to use the combat and shooting skills that he has tried to leave behind.

The mission demonstrates the conflict between John's desire for a peaceful life and his violent past.

## Characters

- John Marston
- Ranch workers
- Pronghorn Ranch residents
- Hostile attackers

## Location

Pronghorn Ranch.

## Rewards

Story progression and continued access to the Epilogue Part 1 storyline.

## Mission Outcome

John protects the ranch but once again reveals the skills that connect him to his former outlaw life.

## Branch Relationship

This mission is part of the main Epilogue Part 1 progression.
