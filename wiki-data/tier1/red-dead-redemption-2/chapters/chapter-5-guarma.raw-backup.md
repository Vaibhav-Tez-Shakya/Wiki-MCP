# Red Dead Redemption 2 — Chapter 5: Guarma

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2
**Chapter:** 5
**Location:** Guarma

After the disastrous Saint Denis bank robbery, Arthur and several members of the gang become stranded on Guarma.

## Missions

1. Welcome to the New World
2. A Kind and Benevolent Despot OR Savagery Unleashed
3. Hell Hath No Fury
4. Paradise Mercifully Departed
5. Dear Uncle Tacitus
6. Fleeting Joy
7. That's Murfree Country OR A Fork in the Road AND Icarus and Friends

**Note:** Icarus and Friends is playable during Chapter 5 but is treated as a Chapter 6 mission when replaying missions.
