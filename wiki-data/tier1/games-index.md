## Structural Similarity

- **DD2, Skyrim, Witcher 3** form a tight cluster: fantasy, open-world, action-RPG, monster contracts/hunts, faction and political-intrigue main stories.
- **DD2 and DL2** both split side content across **two opposing factions/regions** (DD2: Vermund vs. Battahl; DL2: Peacekeepers vs. Survivors) — a shared structural trope even though one is fantasy and the other is zombie-survival.
- **RDR2 and Assassin's Creed IV: Black Flag Resynced** add open-world action-adventure structures to the wiki, with mission progression organized around chapters/sequences.
- **Uncharted 4** is the outlier: no side quests, no open world, a linear third-person-shooter/adventure game — its "quests" are chapters instead.
