# Welcome to the New World

**Game:** Red Dead Redemption 2
**Tier:** 1
**Chapter:** Chapter 5 — Guarma
**Mission Type:** Main Story Mission
**Location:** Guarma
**Branch:** None

## Overview

After the failed Saint Denis bank robbery, Arthur and several members of the gang become stranded on the island of Guarma.

## Objectives

- Survive the shipwreck.
- Explore Guarma.
- Locate the gang members.
- Find a way to escape.

## Walkthrough

Arthur awakens after the shipwreck on Guarma. Separated from much of the gang, he searches for his companions and attempts to understand the island.
